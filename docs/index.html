DarkCircle Suite · Documentation & Guides

Private payments, AMM liquidity, multi-sig custody, a real estate brokerage and grand guild halls on Quai Network (Cyprus-1, chain id 9), behind one real-time 3D portal. These pages are the operator and user manual for the whole suite.

Pre-release

Treat everything here as pre-release. Several apps run as labelled showcases or against simulations, and no contract in the suite has had an independent security audit. Do not point any of it at funds you cannot afford to lose. Details per app: Security & limitations.

The five apps

🛡️

DarkCircle

x402 payment privacy: WQUAI/WQI shielded pools, PPOI compliance, ephemeral payees, a voice agent.
⚡

Circleswap

Quai DeFi dashboard: pool analytics, swap math, LP, and a chain-driven Farms & Pools page.
🔐

DarkSafe

Multi-sig vaults: on-chain proposals, native timelock, quorum checks, extension-only signing.
🏠

Dark Questate

Real estate brokerage: EIP-6065 deeds, broker listings, escrowed offers, one-transaction closings.
⚔️

DarkOrder

Grand Guild Halls: found a guild in one transaction, govern by QNS name, pay x402 tribute, voice seneschal.
