🔒 Security & known limitations

One page for the honest state of the suite: what is audited, what is simulated, and every tracked limitation. These are tracked, not hidden.

Pre-release

No contract in the suite has had an independent security audit. Treat everything as pre-release and do not point any of it at funds you cannot afford to lose.

Known limitations

DarkCircle

  • The pools, relayer and ledger are simulations; nothing is deployed on Quai and no funds move. Gas is not metered except for the Quai revert-burn rule.
  • On static hosting the UI shows a "Static preview" banner, switches its actions off and makes no API calls; the real console is pnpm ui locally.
  • The reference circuit and contract are unaudited, with a single-contributor development ceremony; their behaviour on Quai itself (zone deployment, access lists, the pairing precompile) has not been exercised.
  • Persistence saves the whole simulated environment, keys included, under a passphrase-derived key. It is not a KMS-backed production store; lose the passphrase and the snapshots are unreadable.
  • No Quai-specific sanctions list is bundled (the seed is Ethereum OFAC addresses); load your provider's list at startup.
  • The voice agent understands English commands from a fixed grammar only. On Orchard, WQUAI has no contract, so wrapping and WQUAI sends are refused with ASSET_NOT_ON_NETWORK.

Circleswap

  • On static hosting, explorer-indexed stats and Quainance's catalog are not CORS-open, so USD values come from pool reserves and launches from a dated deploy-time snapshot. Token metadata is unaffected.

DarkSafe

  • The vault contracts have not had an independent audit; the app shows a beta banner.
  • v2 is not deployed yet; until a factory is recorded or configured the app explains that vaults cannot be created or opened.

Dark Questate

  • Contracts not deployed: the hosted site shows invented sample listings with a "Showcase" banner and every transaction switched off.
  • Metadata hosting (publishing the document behind each deed CID) is the registrar's step.
  • Listing reads are unindexed (newest 60 by id). Native QUAI only. Legal effect depends on the operating agreement and local law.

DarkOrder

  • Contracts not deployed: the hosted site shows invented sample halls with a "Showcase" banner and every transaction switched off. QNS names resolve only against a live deployment.

Portal and suite-wide

  • Portal metrics: the hover readout shows the Cyprus-1 block height only; pool TVL and vault status are not wired in.
  • Wallet extensions are treated as untrusted input everywhere: a provider that throws or lacks events is reported as absent.
  • No licence file at the suite root yet; until one is added, reuse rights are unspecified. Vendored three.js keeps its MIT licence in assets/3d/vendor/three/LICENSE (DarkCircle itself is MIT).

Reporting a vulnerability

DarkSafe's reporting policy is in multisigows/SECURITY.md, with its threat model, owner security guide, incident runbook and audit scope under multisigows/docs/. DarkCircle's audit and open items are in x402tumbler/AUDIT.md and its release gates in x402tumbler/docs/SHIELDED_POOL_CHECKLIST.md. Circleswap's internal review is q0/SECURITY_AUDIT.md.
