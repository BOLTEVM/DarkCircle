The WebGL portal

index.html is the real-time 3D front door to the suite. A black obsidian orb hovers inside a crimson-lit octagonal containment vault, wrapped in a ring of plasma. The five apps ride a carousel round the orb; the node at the front haloes the orb and is the selected app.

It opens on Circleswap in front, DarkCircle and DarkSafe to either side, and Dark Questate and DarkOrder behind the orb. Tap the BOLTEVM logo for a new world color.

Graceful degradation

No WebGL, a failed GPU context, a missing model file or prefers-reduced-motion all land on a working 2D portal or a calmer 3D one. The links are plain anchors, so navigation never depends on the 3D engine. In the 2D portal the arrows still work: they move the highlight across the cards, and Enter opens the highlighted app.

Privacy

The portal loads nothing from a third-party host: three.js and every add-on are vendored under assets/3d/vendor/, and there are no web fonts. The only network call is a single read-only quai_blockNumber to rpc.quai.network, made the first time you target a node or spin the carousel, with credentials and referrer omitted. The bottom bar shows its state (RPC idle, querying, CYPRUS-1 #...).
