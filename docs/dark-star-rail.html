🛤️ Dark Star Rail · Talent Escrow Live

The talent agency rail of the DarkCircle suite on Quai Network (Cyprus-1). Clients hire verified AI and human talent into milestone escrow agreements and settle gaslessly over x402: one approval transaction, then an EIP-712 signature — the suite's own facilitator pays the gas. Source: bai/ · Route: /bai/ (alias /darkstarrail/)

Where it stands

The escrow (TalentAgentQuai.vy, Vyper) is deployed and verified on Orchard testnet and mainnet Cyprus-1, with the bridged-USDT settlement token allowlisted and a 2.5% protocol fee. The full gasless flow — signature, settlement, milestone release, fee split — is proven by a live on-chain smoke test. There has been no external audit yet; keep value exposure modest until one lands. Slither, Hardhat, and Foundry fuzz/invariant suites are all green.

How a settlement rides the rail

  • 402 challenge — the backend answers a paid endpoint with a real x402 PAYMENT-REQUIRED challenge naming the escrow, the allowlisted settlement token, and the amount.
  • One approval, then no gas — the client approves the escrow once, then signs an EIP-712 EscrowAuthorization off-chain. Pelagus wallets normalize the signature automatically.
  • Facilitator settles — the suite's own quais.js facilitator verifies the signature, nonce, and time window, then calls create_and_deposit_for, paying the gas. Replay protection comes from a file-backed nonce registry plus on-chain burn.
  • Milestones and fees — each agreement is milestone-scoped; releases pay the talent minus a protocol fee (250 bps, capped at 10%) to the fee recipient. Dispute windows let a silent counterparty's funds be refunded after timeout; pause never traps escrowed funds.

Operational notes

  • The EIP-712 domain name is pinned on-chain as "BTAI Talent Agency" — the former brand. It can never change without redeploying, so the frontend, backend, and facilitator all keep that exact string for signatures.
  • Addresses must be QUAI-ledger Cyprus-1 accounts: second byte below 0x80, or faucets and contracts reject them as Qi UTXO addresses.
  • Never send a legacy QUAI transaction with an explicit empty access list: go-quai's mined execution burns the entire gas limit. Let quais populate a real access list.
  • An hourly on-chain monitor watches both escrows (roles, balance invariant) and pushes a desktop alert only on confirmed drift.
